# Household budget spreadsheet (envelopes by percentage)

A free Excel template for a household budget where each envelope is a share of income instead of a fixed amount. If a month brings in less, every target shrinks with it, so the plan stays right without retyping anything.

It opens in Excel, Google Sheets, LibreOffice and Numbers. Use it, copy it, share it (MIT licence).

- English: [`kinjar-household-budget-template.xlsx`](kinjar-household-budget-template.xlsx)
- Română: [`kinjar-buget-familie-excel.xlsx`](kinjar-buget-familie-excel.xlsx)

Step-by-step guide with a worked example: https://kinjar.app/guides/household-budget-spreadsheet/

## What's in the file

Three sheets:

1. **Start**: how to fill it in. It takes about 15 minutes.
2. **Budget**: the month, your currency, income for everyone who shares the budget, 4 to 6 envelopes with a percentage each, and the categories inside each envelope. A check cell tells you when the percentages don't add up to 100%.
3. **Expenses**: one row per purchase. Pick a category from the drop-down and the envelope fills in by itself; the Budget sheet shows what's left in each envelope.

The example numbers belong to a made-up couple, Emma and James Carter: €5,200 a month split 55% living costs, 20% savings and investing, 15% debt payments and 10% fun and travel. Overwrite them with yours.

## Using it in Google Sheets

Create a blank spreadsheet, choose File > Import > Upload, pick the file and select "Replace spreadsheet". Formulas and drop-downs come across as they are. To budget as a couple, share the sheet with your partner as an editor.

## When the spreadsheet stops being enough

The usual breaking point is typing: every receipt and every bank line by hand, by two people. [Kinjar](https://kinjar.app) is the app we built on the same envelopes-by-percentage method. You photograph receipts or upload a CSV/Excel statement from any bank, the AI turns them into transactions you review, and both partners work in the same ledger. It never asks for a bank login. There's a read-only demo household you can open without an account: https://app.kinjar.app/demo?lang=en

This repository is only the spreadsheet. Nothing in it connects to Kinjar or to any server.
